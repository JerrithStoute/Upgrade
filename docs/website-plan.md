# Website plan — thebuilderstation.com

From a study of jobtread.com (2026-11-05). We borrow their approach, not their words or pictures.

## Our angle
The simpler choice. Both big competitors get called complicated; our pitch is
"construction software you can learn in a day."

## Pages to build first
1. **Home** (outline below)
2. **Pricing** — unlimited users, one price per company, no contract, FAQ that answers objections
3. **Takeoff & plan reading** — our lead feature; short video of Find walls / Find area
4. **Lumber bids** — send lists to suppliers, price snapshots, escalation
5. **QuickBooks** — Desktop *and* Online (rare among competitors)
6. **Spec homes** — break-even, offer calculator, net sheet
7. **Client portal** — your clients see only your company
8. **Switch from CoConstruct** — its users are being pushed off and searching now
9. **Request a demo / Sign up** → app.thebuilderstation.com
10. **About** — the founder story: a working builder who built it for his own company
11. **Terms / Privacy** — waiting on the attorney

## Later
Other feature pages (schedule, daily logs, change orders, selections, invoices, time clock, warranty) ·
"Buildertrend alternative" and "JobTread alternative" pages (positive, never attacking) ·
customer stories (once builders agree) · blog / how-tos · free tools (board-foot calculator,
wall framing calculator, span lookup — JobTread has none, and they lead straight to our takeoff) ·
public help center from our manual.

## Home page, top to bottom
1. **Headline:** construction software you can learn in a day. Sub: takeoff → lumber order → QuickBooks, in one place. Buttons: Watch a 3-minute tour · Get a demo
2. **Trust line:** built by a working builder (review counts once we have them)
3. **Why it's simpler** — 3 cards: unlimited users, one price · no email noise, every email is your choice · the same buttons work the same everywhere
4. **Takeoff:** plans in, walls and rooms found, material list out
5. **Lumber bids and price protection**
6. **QuickBooks Desktop + Online:** bills in, invoices out, payments back
7. **Your clients see only you** (branded client app)
8. **Spec homes**
9. **Owner pay stays private**
10. **Pricing teaser:** flat, unlimited users
11. **Switching help:** we bring in your lists; nothing has to be loaded up front
12. **Founder story** + closing button

## What JobTread does well (copy the idea)
- One page per feature — each one is something people find on Google
- Price shown openly; FAQ answers every objection
- Risk taken away: no contract, no setup fee, free import and training, money-back guarantee
- Fear of switching handled head-on: how long it takes, run both side by side, a person who helps
- Two buttons everywhere: a soft one (watch / demo) and a hard one (sign up)
- Founder story with real numbers
- Competitor pages that stay positive

## Don't copy
- A giant 30-feature menu (says "complicated") — keep the menu short: Features, Pricing, Switch, About
- Badge walls and big usage numbers we don't have yet — honest proof only
- Per-user price calculator — we're unlimited users
- Same blocks repeated on every page — keep pages short
- AI / formula promotion; hype words ("elevate", "next level") — plain builder talk
- Their text, photos, videos, taglines

## Decisions only you can make before writing
- Tier prices
- Free trial or not
- Money-back guarantee or not
